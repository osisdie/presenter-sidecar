# Weekly sync: Plant-care team

Fictional meeting notes. Each `##` section with a "Suggested time" line is one timer stage
(`pacing_notes` in the config), so the notes are also the agenda.

## Part 1: Check-in

Suggested time: ~5 min

- One sentence each: what you finished, what blocks you.

## Part 2: Board walk

Suggested time: ~15 min

**1. In progress**

- Oldest card first; ask what would finish it this week.

**2. Blocked**

- Name an owner for each blocker before moving on.

## Part 3: Release

Suggested time: ~10 min

- Walk the release checklist; anything unchecked gets a date.

## Part 4: Wrap-up

Suggested time: ~5 min

- Repeat the owners and dates out loud.

## Appendix: links

- Not a stage (no suggested time): board, checklist, last week's notes.
